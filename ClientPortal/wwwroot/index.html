AgriConnect Service-Oriented Platform

Select a user role below to access its dedicated interface and backend microservice.

1. Farmer Portal

Register farmer details, submit supply requests, compare quotations, confirm orders, and post harvested crops.

Service Port: 5001 (farmer_data.db)

2. Supplier Portal

Manage warehouse stock, view farmer supply requests, send price quotations, and update delivery status.

Service Port: 5002 (supplier_data.db)

3. Agriculture Officer

Verify supply requests and quotations, approve or reject orders, and publish farming recommendations.

Service Port: 5003 (officer_data.db)

4. Administration

Manage user accounts, check microservice status, and monitor system-wide audit logs.

Service Port: 5004 (admin_data.db)
