AgriConnect Service-Oriented Platform

Select a user role below to access its portal. Each role runs as an independent .NET microservice with its own database under Services/*/Data/, routed through this ClientApp gateway.

Checking live microservices...

1. Farmer Portal

Register farmer profiles, submit supply requests, compare supplier quotations, confirm orders, track deliveries, read officer advisories, and post harvested crops.

Service: localhost:5001 • DB: farmer_db.json

2. Supplier Portal

Manage warehouse stock, view open farmer supply requests, submit price quotations, update delivery statuses, and browse available crops.

Service: localhost:5002 • DB: supplier_db.json

3. Agriculture Officer

Verify supply requests and supplier quotations, approve or reject orders with compliance notes, and publish farming advisories for farmers.

Service: localhost:5003 • DB: officer_db.json

4. Administration

Manage platform user accounts, monitor live service health across all 4 microservices, and inspect system-wide audit logs.

Service: localhost:5004 • DB: admin_db.json
